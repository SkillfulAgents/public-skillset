# Hook Database

Consult for ALL hook titles, opening lines, and variants. The first second decides whether the ad gets watched: **title on screen + first spoken words + a face already talking**, all inside ~1s. Hook codes are the tracker's `hook` axis — use them exactly.

## Hook codes

| Code | Archetype | Shape | Example (generic SaaS) |
|---|---|---|---|
| hpain | Call out the pain | Name the manual/annoying thing, imperative | "Stop chasing invoices by hand" |
| hclaim | Bold claim | Product did something big for me | "This replaced my whole ops team" |
| hcuriosity | Open a loop | Withhold the how, promise a reveal | "I gave it my inbox. Watch what happened." |
| hnumber | Concrete result | Specific count + time frame | "Eleven hours back this week. Here's how." |
| hpov | Relatable scenario | "POV:" + a moment the viewer recognizes | "POV: it's 9am and the reports are already done" |
| hcontra | Contrarian take | Attack the category / default belief | "Spreadsheets are dead. This is different." |
| hreply | Reply to a comment | Comment card on frame 1, answer it | "@sam: how do you find leads so fast??" |
| hcompare | Us vs them | Name the alternative's failure, then ours | "Other tools give up here. Mine doesn't." |
| hconfession | Confession / mistake | Admit the thing everyone does wrong | "I wasted a year doing this manually" |
| hquestion | Direct question | Question the viewer answers "yes" to | "Still copying data between tabs?" |
| hwarning | Warning | Consequence-first | "Don't launch another ad without this" |
| hsecret | Insider / hack | "Nobody tells you…", "Steal this" | "Steal this workflow before it's everywhere" |
| hstory | Time-stamped anecdote | Specific moment → outcome | "2am, a support ticket comes in. By 8 it's solved." |
| hlist | Countdown / N things | "3 things I automated this week" | "3 things I never do manually anymore" |
| hbefore | Before / after | The old way, the new way, fast | "Before: 40 tabs. After: one message." |
| hdemo | Show, don't tell | Product visual first, VO explains | (full-bleed UI on frame 1) |
| hwhat | "What if" | Hypothetical the product makes real | "What if your CRM updated itself?" |
| hunpopular | Unpopular opinion | Signals a take | "Unpopular opinion: you don't need a bigger team" |
| htut | Tutorial | "How to X in 10 seconds" | "How I set up an AI coworker in ten seconds" |
| hgenz | Fast casual quick hit | Short, slangy, meme-adjacent, 8s total | "Stop chasing leads. Seriously." |

## Templates (fill with company.md facts)

- `Stop <manual thing> by hand`
- `I <verb> <count> <things> in <time>. Here's how.`
- `POV: <moment> and <outcome already happened>`
- `<Category default> is dead. This is different.`
- `I gave <product> my <asset>. Watch what happened.`
- `Other <category tools> <fail here>. <Product> doesn't.`
- `Steal this <workflow / setup>`
- `What if your <system> <did the thing itself>?`

## Benchmarks (short-form paid social, 2026)

- 3-second hold ("hook rate") — good ≥ 30%, great ≥ 40%. Below 20% = hook problem, not ad problem.
- Sub-9s ads get a reach penalty on most feeds; aim 10–15s.
- Hook title ≤ 6 words per line, max 2 lines, sentence case.
- Presenter should already be mid-motion at frame 0 (no settle-in).

## Fatigued patterns — avoid

- "Wait for it…" / "You won't believe…" with no payoff structure
- "As a <role>…" openers
- "Hey guys" / "So today I'm going to…"
- "This app is INSANE / a game changer" (no claim, no specificity)
- Robotic ALL-CAPS single-word titles ("GAME CHANGER")
- Stock B-roll before the face appears
- Clapping / pointing at floating text
- Any hook that takes longer than 1.5s to reach the promise

## Guardrails (FTC / platform)

- Every claim must be true for the company AND plausible for a real user to say. No invented statistics — numbers come from `company.md` or the user, or are framed as the presenter's own experience ("I").
- AI presenters are fine for paid ads; do not present them as real customer testimonials ("real customer", "verified review"). Use first-person experience framing, not "reviews".
- No "guaranteed", "cures", "#1" without substantiation. No competitor disparagement beyond factual category critique.
- Health / finance / legal categories: soften to "helped me", "for me"; confirm claims with the user.
- Include the brand name once in VO, and the CTA URL in the outro card.

## Variant strategy

Vary ONE axis at a time off an anchor: same script + new hook code is the cheapest test (only clip A changes, clip B shared). Record hook-rate per code in ADS.md; the next batch iterates on the winning code.
