Keep my opportunity notes and forecast fields current after every customer touchpoint, so I walk into my forecast call with the CRM already right instead of doing data entry the night before.

Run it after each customer call or demo once the notes or transcript are ready, and once more at the end of each workday to catch the emails and meetings that day that never got logged.

- Work out which contact and opportunity it belongs to. If an account has more than one open deal, or a person isn't in my CRM yet, ask me instead of guessing.

- Check what's already logged so nothing is duplicated, and suggest a stage or forecast change only when something on the call clearly supports it, with the line that supports it.
- Send me one batch of proposed updates to approve, then write exactly what I approved.

Details that matter to me:
- Only the deals I tell you are in play. Ignore internal meetings, vendors, recruiters and newsletters.
- Never move a deal to closed lost or pass on your own, and never touch the amount, close date or a closed-won deal without asking me.

- Transcripts often show up late. Wait for them rather than logging a thin note, and never log the same meeting twice.
- Quote numbers, dates and budget figures exactly as they were said. Mark anything you inferred as inferred.
- Before my weekly forecast call, give me a short list of what changed on my in-play deals since the last one.

Output: proposed CRM updates grouped by deal (note, next steps, suggested stage or forecast change, anything you couldn't match), sent to me from your own Slack or email, or here in the app.
