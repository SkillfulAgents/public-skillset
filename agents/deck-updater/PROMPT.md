Set up an agent that turns each of my demo and discovery calls into a tailored follow-up deck, so I walk into the next conversation with something the prospect will actually read instead of our generic master deck.

Run it whenever the notes or transcript from one of my external sales calls are ready. If the notes show up late, check through the workday and pick them up, but never build two decks for the same call.

When it runs:
- Read the call and pull out the prospect's goals, their pains in their own words, the use cases they cared about, the objections they raised, who was in the room and their roles, and the next steps we agreed.
- Make a copy of my master deck (never touch the original) and tailor the copy: a short recap of the call, how we map to their specific pains, the proof points and examples that fit their situation, pricing only if it came up, the agreed next steps, and one clear call to action.
- Reuse our standard slides where they fit, cut the ones that don't, and keep the copy short.
- File it in a folder for that account with a predictable name.
- Draft a short covering email in my voice that references the call and the next step and links the deck, in my own email drafts, on the existing thread with them if there is one.
- If my next meeting with them is already on the calendar, make sure the deck is ready before it.

Details that matter to me:
- Keep our theme, fonts and layouts. Don't restyle anything.
- Only use proof points, customer names, logos and numbers from material I've approved. Never invent a metric or a reference customer.
- Mark everything you inferred, as opposed to what was actually said on the call, so I can check it before it goes out.
- Quote their numbers, dates and names exactly as they said them.
- Skip internal meetings, recruiting calls and anything that isn't a sales conversation.
- Don't change who the deck is shared with until I say so.

Output: a tailored draft deck in my slides, a covering email waiting in my drafts, and a short note to me with both links and the list of things to double-check.

My default: build the deck and draft the email on your own, but never send the email or share the deck with anyone outside my company. I review and send everything.

Test it on one recorded call first so I can see the result before you switch it on.

Tailor this to the tools I actually use and ask me the critical questions first: which tools, what the output looks like, and what you do on your own vs. check with me.
