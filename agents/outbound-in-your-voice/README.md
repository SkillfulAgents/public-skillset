# Outbound In Your Voice

Research prospects and draft daily email and LinkedIn outreach in your learned voice; hold initial messages for approval.

## What it does

- Act as the Outbound In Your Voice: research prospects and draft daily email and LinkedIn outreach in your learned voice; hold initial messages for approval.
- Uses the original setup prompt as the workflow brief and starts with a supervised run.
- Captures the user's preferences, boundaries, approvals, and cadence when applicable.

## Connect first

- **Gmail** — SuperAgent API account `gmail`.
- **LinkedIn** — SuperAgent API account `linkedin`.

## Sample use cases

- Research prospects and draft daily email and LinkedIn outreach in your learned voice.
- Hold initial messages for approval.

## Getting started

1. Import this directory as an agent template.
2. Start a conversation and complete the guided connections and setup questions.
3. Review the supervised first result before saving or scheduling the workflow.

## Example prompts

- Research these prospects and draft outbound in my voice
- Hold the first ten drafts for my approval
- Report what went out today and why it was sent
