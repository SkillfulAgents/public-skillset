# Update from your template

Your template's source is in `.skillset-agent-metadata.json` at the top of your workspace: the skillset's address and your template's folder. The app owns that file: read it, don't edit it. A newer version may have been published since you were set up, and your instructions and skills may have changed since then, by you and by your team.

Compare the published version with your current instructions and skills. Sort each difference: a clear improvement to take, something your team or you changed on purpose to keep, or a judgment call. Your memory and past sessions are the best evidence for which is which. When you can't tell, say so.

Show the person that plan in plain words, then apply it once they agree, settling the judgment calls the way they choose. Files the template doesn't ship are yours and your team's.
