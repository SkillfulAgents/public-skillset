# Reply assessment rubric

## The scale (fixed; do not drift between runs)

The score is the likelihood that this candidate takes a screening call in the next few weeks, judged from the reply plus context.

| Band | Score | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Interested | 80–100 | Proposes times, asks for the JD or a call, shares availability or a phone number, says "yes, tell me more". |
| Warm / ambiguous | 40–79 | Asks questions first (comp range, remote policy, team size), "maybe, depends on…", "send me details", slow but engaged. |
| Polite decline | 10–39 | "Happy where I am", "not looking right now", "reach out in six months", a referral to someone else instead. |
| Hard decline | 0–9 | Firm no, irritation, "please remove me", "do not contact". |

Placing within a band:
- Start at the band midpoint (90, 60, 25, 5).
- Move up for: a concrete question about the role, a specific timeframe, a reply within a day, a known push factor (recent layoff or reorg at their company, long tenure, a stated wish for a change).
- Move down for: a one-line reply, hedges ("not actively looking but…" stays warm, but low warm), a recent job change (under 9 months), a stated constraint the role cannot meet (on-site vs. remote, a comp floor above the band).
- Never leave a band because of research. Research changes fit, not the reply's intent; put fit in Strengths and Gaps.
- "Do not contact" in any wording is 0, whatever else the message says.

Not replies (skip the assessment, log in the ledger): auto-replies, out-of-office, bounces, calendar acceptances with no text, and messages from someone other than the candidate (an assistant, a recruiter at their company).

## The draft (addressed to the user only, same subject, under ~400 words)

```
[If applicable, first line:] DO NOT CONTACT: <candidate> asked not to be contacted again. Remove from all outreach and sequences.

Likelihood to screen: <score>/100 (<band>)

Reply summary: <2–3 sentences: what they said, the tone, any question or condition they raised.>

Profile: <current title> at <company>, <tenure if known>, <location>. Profile: <URL from search results, or "not found">.
Match confidence: <high/medium/low and why>.

Strengths for <role> at <client/company>:
- <evidence-based point>
- <evidence-based point>

Gaps / risks:
- <point, e.g. 5 months into current role; comp floor likely above band>

Suggested next step: <one concrete action, e.g. "Answer the comp question with the band and offer two times this week">.

Sources consulted: Web search (found: <what>), email history (<n> prior threads / none), ATS (<record found / not in ATS>), Drive (<found / nothing>), Slack (<found / nothing / not connected>).
```

Rules:
- Facts in Profile and Strengths must come from a named source. Unknown fields say "unknown".
- Don't quote the candidate at length; a short phrase in quotes is fine when it sets the score.
- No protected characteristics, no guesses about age or family situation.
- Before saving: To = the user's own address only, Cc and Bcc empty, subject identical to the thread's (with "Re:"), threading headers set.
