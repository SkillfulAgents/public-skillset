# Update structure and checklist

Use the user's own structure when a prior update exists. Use this one only for a first update, or to fill a section the user's format lacks.

## Startup monthly update (default)

1. **Subject**: `<Company> update: <Month YYYY>` (or the user's pattern).
2. **Headline** (1-2 sentences): the single most important thing, good or bad.
3. **Key metrics** (table or short list), each with: this period, last period, change. Typical set: MRR or ARR, net new revenue, customers or logos, a usage or activation metric, gross margin if reported, net burn, cash, runway in months.
4. **Wins**: shipped launches, signed customers, hires. Specific and named where allowed.
5. **Misses and lowlights**: what fell short, why, and the plan.
6. **Last period's commitments**: each one, quoted briefly, with done / in progress / dropped (reason).
7. **Asks**: 1-3, each specific enough that an investor can act today (who, what, by when).
8. **Thanks**: people who helped on last period's asks.

## LP update (quarterly)

Headline; fund status as reported by the fund admin (never computed); capital called and distributed (confirm); new investments and follow-ons; portfolio highlights and lowlights; exits or markdowns (needs your call); team and firm news; asks (co-investors, intros). All performance figures come from source documents or are `(needs number)`.

## Partner-meeting recap

Decisions made (with owner); pipeline movement (new, advanced, passed, with one-line reason); portfolio issues needing partner attention; follow-ups with owner and date; items carried from last recap.

## Placeholders

- `(needs number)`: a value you could not find or could not reconcile. Add a short hint: `(needs number: Stripe shows $41.2k, sheet shows $43.0k)`.
- `(needs your call)`: wording or disclosure that is a judgment (fundraising status, departures, NDA customers).

## Checklist before handoff

- [ ] A draft for this period did not already exist.
- [ ] Every metric from last update appears, or its removal is explained.
- [ ] Each metric uses the same definition as last time, or the change is stated.
- [ ] Every "next period" commitment and open ask has a status.
- [ ] Headline is in the first two lines.
- [ ] Every number traces to a source listed in the summary. None are estimated without a label.
- [ ] Percentages are not used on tiny bases.
- [ ] Sensitive items are flagged, not included.
- [ ] Asks are specific and addressable.
- [ ] Length and tone match the user's last 2-3 updates.
- [ ] Saved as a draft. Not sent. No recipients added unless configured.
- [ ] Summary lists the headline, all placeholders, the open asks and the sources used.
