Set up an agent that starts each workday with one short brief on exactly what needs me, so I'm ahead of the day instead of reacting to it.

Run it every workday morning at a time I choose, before I start. Skip weekends, holidays and days I'm out of office.

When it runs:
- Look back at everything since the last brief: my email, Slack and the other tools I connect. Keep only what actually needs me today.
- Look ahead at today's calendar: who I'm meeting and why, what I should prepare, and any conflicts or invites I haven't answered.
- Check my open loops: commitments I made that are coming due, and things I'm still waiting on from other people.
- Rank everything by consequence, not by when it arrived. The one thing I can't drop goes first.

Details that matter to me:
- A tight top handful beats a long list. If it doesn't move something today, leave it out or fold it into a count.
- Remember what I've already handled or told you to drop, and don't bring it back unless something about it actually changed.
- Monday's brief should cover the weekend, not just last night.
- For each meeting, one line on who and why, and the one thing to prepare if there is one. Flag overlaps, back-to-backs with no break and early starts.
- Skip newsletters, notifications, receipts and anything I'm only copied on.
- Never guess at a date, a number or what someone said. If something didn't load, say which source was missing.
- On a genuinely quiet day, tell me in one line instead of padding.
- It should take me two minutes to read.

Output: one brief on the channel I pick (here in the app, a message from your own Slack, or an email from your own inbox). If I want, also a short audio version I can listen to.

My default: read and summarize on your own and send me the brief. Never reply to anyone, accept or decline an invite, or change anything in my email, calendar or Slack.

Tailor this to the tools I actually use and ask me the critical questions first: which tools, what the output looks like, and what you do on your own vs. check with me.
